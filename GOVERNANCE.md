# Governance

Adslop is maintained by Mikko Kotila (`@mikkokotila`).

The maintainer decides product scope, issue priority, release timing, and who may contribute or review. These decisions do not waive mechanical gates or independent review requirements.

The repository constitution is [CLAUDE.md](CLAUDE.md). The configured required gates, its annotated laws, and the checked-in ruleset must agree. Changing enforcement requires a coherent, reviewed change to the contract and its implementation.

## Declared policy and live enforcement

[SETUP.md](SETUP.md) records activation requirements and observed blockers. A ruleset file does not establish that the corresponding protection is active on GitHub. Missing platform capability, credentials, or an eligible reviewer must remain visible and cannot be represented as completed enforcement.

[MAINTAINERS.md](MAINTAINERS.md) explains ownership and independent review. GitHub's actual rules and eligible reviews determine whether its approval requirements are satisfied; a maintainer name in prose is not an identity restriction enforced by GitHub.

## Decision records

Material product and governance decisions should be traceable through slice issues, pull requests, release notes, or canonical documentation. [SPECIFICATION.md](SPECIFICATION.md) records product intent; implementation and validation records establish what the software currently does.
